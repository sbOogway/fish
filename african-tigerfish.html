Distribution

African Tigerfish inhabits Freshwater waters. Map shows fishbase occurrence records.

Description

Hydrocynus vittatus, the African tigerfish, tiervis or ngwesh is a freshwater fish distributed throughout much of Africa. This predatory fish is generally a piscivore but it has been observed leaping out of the water and catching barn swallows in flight.

Size

Maximum recorded length is 105 cm.

Weight

Maximum recorded weight is 28.0 kg.

Age

Lifespan is around 8 years.

Biology

Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Oct, Nov, Dec. Its diet consists mainly of Bony fish, Insects and shrimps & prawns.

Seasonality

Its most productive fishing months in the Northern Hemisphere are typically Jan, Feb, Mar, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec, though exact timing depends on local climate and water temperature.

Conservation

IUCN conservation status: LC.
