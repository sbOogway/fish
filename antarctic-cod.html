Distribution

Description

The marbled rockcod is a species of marine ray-finned fish, belonging to the family Nototheniidae, the notothens or cod icefishes. It is native to the Southern Ocean, where it can be found at depths from 5 to 350 m. This is a commercially important species.

Biology

Mature females may spawn for the first time from around 6-8 years of age . Spawning occurs mainly in May, Jun. Fecundity ranges from 19,271-109,710 eggs. Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Ascidians, Bony fish, Euphausiids, Gastropods, Isopods, Polychaetes and shrimps & prawns. It may be targeted by predators such as Diomedea melanophrys.

Behavior

Immature and mature fish are benthic but stay in shallower depths (10-20 m). Spawn once a year .
