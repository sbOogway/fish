Distribution

Atlantic Bonito inhabits Brackish, Saltwater waters. Records span a broad geographic range. Map shows fishbase occurrence records.

Description

The Atlantic bonito is a large mackerel-like fish of the family Scombridae. It is common in shallow waters of the Atlantic Ocean, the Mediterranean Sea, and the Black Sea, where it is an important commercial and game fish.

Size

Typical length is 50 cm, with a maximum recorded length of 91 cm.

Weight

Maximum recorded weight is 11.0 kg.

Age

Lifespan is around 5 years.

Sexual maturity is reached at about 1 years of age.

Biology

Spawning occurs mainly in Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul. Fecundity ranges from 44,620-446,530 eggs. Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Bony fish, Euphausiids, Shrimps & prawns and squids/cuttlefish. It may be targeted by predators such as Acanthocybium solandri, Coryphaena hippurus and Sarda sarda.

Seasonality

Its most productive fishing months in the Northern Hemisphere are typically Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec, though exact timing depends on local climate and water temperature.

Conservation

IUCN conservation status: LC.
