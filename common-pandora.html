Distribution

Description

The common pandora, also known as king of the breams, pandora, red pandora or Spanish seabream, is a species of marine ray-finned fish belonging to the family Sparidae, which includes the seabreams and porgies. This fish is found in the eastern Atlantic Ocean and the Mediterranean Sea. It is an esteemed fish to eat.

Biology

Possibly two spawning periods in the southern Mediterranean . Length at which sex change possibly occurs is between 12.8 and 20.3 cm FL . Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec. Fecundity ranges from 22,200-151,000 eggs. Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Bivalves, Bony fish, Crabs, Gastropods, Isopods, Mysids, Non-annelids, Ostracods, Polychaetes, Sea cucumbers, Sea stars/brittle stars, Shrimps & prawns, Squids/cuttlefish and stomatopods. It may be targeted by predators such as Mustelus mustelus and Seriola dumerili.

Behavior

Found on inshore waters, on various bottom (rock, gravel, sand and mud) to 200 m (Mediterranean) or 300 m (Atlantic) and move to deeper waters during winter.
