## Description
The vundu is a species of large airbreathing catfish found widely in rivers and other freshwater habitats of sub-Saharan Africa, as well as the Nile. It is also called the sampa, cur, lenda, or certa.

## Biology
Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep. Fecundity ranges from 6,001-51,216 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Bony fish, Carcasses, Debris, Insects and shore birds.
It may be targeted by predators such as Bufo regularis.
