## Description
Aulostomus maculatus, the West Atlantic trumpetfish, is a long-bodied fish with an upturned mouth. It often swims vertically while trying to blend with vertical coral, such as sea rods, sea pens, and pipe sponges.

## Biology
Oviparous, gonochorous, with pelagic eggs .
Its diet consists mainly of Bony fish and fish eggs & larvae.
It may be targeted by predators such as Cephalopholis fulva, Epinephelus guttatus, Lutjanus apodus, Lutjanus jocu and Mycteroperca venenosa.
