## Description
The Atlantic horse mackerel, also known as the European horse mackerel or common scad, is a species of jack mackerel in the family Carangidae, which includes the jacks, pompanos and trevallies. It is found in the eastern Atlantic Ocean off Europe and Africa and into the south-eastern Indian Ocean. It is an important species in commercial fisheries and is listed as a Vulnerable species on The IUCN Red List of Threatened Species.

## Biology
Spawning occurs mainly in Apr, May, Jun, Jul. Fecundity ranges from 19,225-740,000 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Ascidians, Benthic algae/weeds, Bivalves, Bony fish, Copepods, Crabs, Euphausiids, Fish eggs & larvae, Gastropods, Jellyfish/hydroids, Lobsters, Mysids, Ostracods, Polychaetes, Sea stars/brittle stars, Sea urchins, Shrimps & prawns, Sponges and squids/cuttlefish.
It may be targeted by predators such as Arctocephalus pusillus pusillus, Atractoscion aequidens, Chelidonichthys gurnardus, Coryphaena hippurus, Merlangius merlangus, Merluccius capensis, Merluccius merluccius, Merluccius paradoxus, Phocoena phocoena, Raja alba, Sarda sarda, Squalus acanthias, Uranoscopus scaber and Zeus faber.
