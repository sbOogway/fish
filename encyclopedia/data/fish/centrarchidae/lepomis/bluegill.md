## Description
The bluegill, sometimes referred to as "bream", "brim", "sunny", or, in Texas, "copper nose", is a species of North American freshwater fish, native to and commonly found in streams, rivers, lakes, ponds and wetlands east of the Rocky Mountains. It is from the family Centrarchidae in the order Centrarchiformes.

## Biology
Males guard the eggs for about 7 days .
Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Dec.
Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Bony fish, Gastropods, Insects, Non-annelids and shrimps & prawns.
It may be targeted by predators such as Cichla ocellaris, Dolomedes sp., Esox lucius, Esox masquinongy, Micropterus salmoides, Morone saxatilis and Salmo trutta trutta.
