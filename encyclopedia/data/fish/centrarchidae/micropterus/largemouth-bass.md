## Description
The largemouth bass is a carnivorous, freshwater, ray-finned fish in the Centrarchidae (sunfish) family, native to the eastern and central United States, southeastern Canada and northern Mexico. It is known by a variety of regional names, such as the widemouth bass, bigmouth bass, black bass, largie, potter's fish, Florida bass or Florida largemouth, green bass, bucketmouth bass, green trout, growler, Gilsdorf bass, Oswego bass, southern largemouth, northern largemouth, Alabama bass or Alabama largemouth, marsh bass, and swamp bass.

## Biology
The male which becomes aggressive and territorial builds the nest on muddy bottoms of shallow water. A female may spawn with several males on different nests. The male guards and fans the eggs for about 29 days . Spawning takes place spring to summer or when temperature reaches 15°C. Adults mate between the age of 5-12 years . Also Ref, 88808).
Spawning occurs mainly in Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul. Fecundity ranges from 4,550-54,732 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Benth. copepods, Benthic algae/weeds, Bony fish, Cladocerans, Copepods, Fish eggs & larvae, Gastropods, Insects, Lobsters, Ostracods, Shore birds, Shrimps & prawns and toads/frogs.
It may be targeted by predators such as Argyrosomus hololepidotus, Esox lucius, Esox masquinongy, Ichthyomyzon castaneus, Micropterus salmoides, Perca flavescens, Ptychocheilus oregonensis and Sander vitreus.
