## Description
Hyperoglyphe antarctica, the Antarctic butterfish, bluenose warehou, deepsea trevally, blue eye trevalla, blue-eye cod, bluenose sea bass, or deep sea trevalla, is a medusafish of the family Centrolophidae found in all the southern oceans, at depths of between 40 and 1,500 m. Its length is up to about 140 cm, with a maximum published weight of 60 kg.

## Biology
In Australia, spawning appears to be correlated with water temperature and nutrient upwellings . It appears that mature fish move up the continental slope into shallow depths (320-400 m) and aggregate in specific grounds for spawning . There is no information on the number of eggs blue eye produce nor on the egg and larval stages of their life history .
Spawning occurs mainly in Mar, Apr, May. Fecundity ranges from 1,300,000-11,000,000 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Ascidians, Bony fish and squids/cuttlefish.
It may be targeted by predators such as Orcinus orca.
