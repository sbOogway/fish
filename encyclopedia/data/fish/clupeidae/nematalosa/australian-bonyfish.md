## Description
Bony bream Nematalosa erebi are a widespread and common, small to medium-sized Australian freshwater fish often found in large shoals throughout much of northern and central Australia, and the Murray-Darling basin.

## Biology
In more northerly part of its range, spawning may take place several times over the year but is probably annual in southern localities.
Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Oct, Nov, Dec. Fecundity ranges from 33,000-880,000 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Benthic algae/weeds, Debris and insects.
It may be targeted by predators such as Argyrosomus hololepidotus, Arius sp., Haliastur sphenurus, Lates calcarifer, Maccullochella peelii, Macquaria ambigua, Milvus migrans, Pelecanus conspicillatus, Perca fluviatilis and Phalacrocorax sp..
