## Description
The Peruvian anchoveta is a species of fish of the anchovy family, Engraulidae, from the Southeast Pacific Ocean. It is one of the most commercially important fish species in the world, with annual harvests varying between 3.14 and 8.32 million tonnes from 2010 to 2021.

## Biology
Eggs ellipsoidal.
Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec. Fecundity ranges from 12,701-18,495 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Ascidians, Bivalves, Cladocerans, Copepods, Diatoms, Dinoflagellates, Euphausiids, Fish eggs & larvae, Green algae, Isopods, Mysids, Ostracods and polychaetes.
It may be targeted by predators such as Arctocephalus australis, Bigua cormorant, Lagenorhynchus obscurus, Larosterna inca, Larus pipixcan, Merluccius gayi gayi, Merluccius gayi peruanus, Otaria flavescens, Pelecanus occidentalis thagus, Pelecanus thagus, Phalacrocorax bougainvillii, Phalacrocorax brasilianus, Phalacrocorax gaimardi, Puffinus griseus, Sarda chiliensis chiliensis, Scomber japonicus, Spheniscus humboldti, Sula nebouxii, Sula variegata, Trachurus murphyi and Trachurus symmetricus.
