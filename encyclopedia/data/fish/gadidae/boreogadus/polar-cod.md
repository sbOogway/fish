## Description
Boreogadus saida, known as the polar cod or as the Arctic cod, is a fish of the cod family Gadidae, related to the true cod. Another fish species for which both the common names Arctic cod and polar cod are used is Arctogadus glacialis.

## Biology
Oviparous, sexes are separate . Fish move close inshore, often in large numbers to spawn .
Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Nov, Dec. Fecundity ranges from 9,000-67,000 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Ascidians, Benth. copepods, Bony fish, Carcasses, Cladocerans, Copepods, Crabs, Diatoms, Euphausiids, Fish eggs & larvae, Gastropods, Isopods, Mysids, Ostracods, Polychaetes, Shrimps & prawns and sponges.
It may be targeted by predators such as Balaenoptera acutorostrata, Boreogadus saida, Cystophora cristata, Delphinapterus leucas, Fulmarus glacialis, Gadus ogac, Hippoglossoides platessoides, Illex illecebrosus, Micromesistius poutassou, Pagophilus groenlandicus, Phoca groenlandica, Pusa hispida, Reinhardtius hippoglossoides, Uria aalge, Uria lomvia and narwhals.
