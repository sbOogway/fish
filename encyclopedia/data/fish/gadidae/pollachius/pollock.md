## Description
Pollachius pollachius is a species of marine fish in the family Gadidae. FAO uses the English name pollack for this species, whereas in American English it is known as European pollock. Other vernacular names include lythe, and in the Isle of Man, callig.

## Biology
Oviparous and gonochorous .
Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May.
Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Benth. copepods, Bony fish, Cladocerans, Copepods, Crabs, Euphausiids, Fish eggs & larvae, Jellyfish/hydroids, Polychaetes, Shrimps & prawns and squids/cuttlefish.
It may be targeted by predators such as Myxine glutinosa.
