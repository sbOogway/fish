## Description
Scarus iseri is a species of fish of the family Scaridae. Its common names include striped parrotfish. The species was described by Bloch in 1789.

## Biology
Diandric. Length at sex change = 9.6 cm TL . Forms harem groups composed of a single male and several smaller females .
It may be targeted by predators such as Caranx ruber, Epinephelus striatus, Lutjanus griseus, Mycteroperca interstitialis and Mycteroperca venenosa.
