## Description
The schoolmaster snapper, is a species of marine ray-finned fish; a snapper belonging to the family Lutjanidae. It is found in the western Atlantic Ocean, and like other snapper species, it is a popular food fish.

## Biology
Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun.
Its diet consists mainly of Crabs, Gastropods, Non-annelids, Shrimps & prawns and squids/cuttlefish.
