European seabass, also known as branzino, European bass, sea bass, common bass, white bass, capemouth, white salmon, white mullet, sea dace, levrek or loup de mer, is a primarily ocean-going fish native to the waters off Europe's western and southern and Africa's northern coasts, though it can also be found in shallow coastal waters and river mouths during the summer months and late autumn. It is one of only six species in its family, Moronidae, collectively called the temperate basses.

## Habitat
European Seabass is a saltwater (sea and brackish water) species. It is targeted across much of its range, with fishing conditions varying by season and locality.

## Seasonality
Its most productive fishing months in the Northern Hemisphere are typically Mar, Apr, May, Sep, Oct, Nov, though exact timing depends on local climate and water temperature.
