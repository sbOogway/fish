The Patagonian toothfish, also known as Chilean sea bass and mero, is a species of notothen found in cold waters between depths of 45 and 3,850 m in the southern Atlantic, Pacific, and Indian Oceans and Southern Ocean on seamounts and continental shelves around most Subantarctic islands.

## Habitat
Patagonian Toothfish is a saltwater (sea and brackish water) species. It is targeted across much of its range, with fishing conditions varying by season and locality.
