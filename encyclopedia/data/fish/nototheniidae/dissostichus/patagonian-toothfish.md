## Description
The Patagonian toothfish, also known as Chilean sea bass and mero, is a species of notothen found in cold waters between depths of 45 and 3,850 m in the southern Atlantic, Pacific, and Indian Oceans and Southern Ocean on seamounts and continental shelves around most Subantarctic islands.

## Biology
May spawn for the first time from around 8-10 years of age .
Spawning occurs mainly in May, Jul, Aug, Sep. Fecundity ranges from 238,314-545,665 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Bony fish, Euphausiids, Octopi, Polychaetes, Shrimps & prawns and squids/cuttlefish.
It may be targeted by predators such as Arctocephalus gazella, Bathyraja albomaculata, Bathyraja brachyurops, Bathyraja griseocauda, Cottoperca gobio, Diomedea melanophrys, Dissostichus eleginoides and Salilota australis.
