## Description
The summer flounder or fluke is a marine flatfish that is found in the Atlantic Ocean off the East Coast of the United States and Canada. It is especially abundant in waters from North Carolina to Massachusetts.

## Biology
Distinct pairing .
Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Dec.
Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Benth. copepods, Benthic algae/weeds, Bivalves, Bony fish, Copepods, Crabs, Mysids, Polychaetes and shrimps & prawns.
It may be targeted by predators such as Cynoscion regalis, Morone saxatilis, Pomatomus saltator, Prionotus carolinus and Squalus acanthias.
