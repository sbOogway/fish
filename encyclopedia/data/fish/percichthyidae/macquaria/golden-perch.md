## Description
The golden perch is a medium-sized, yellow or gold-coloured species of Australian freshwater fish found primarily in the Murray-Darling River system. A subspecies is found in the Lake Eyre-Cooper Creek system, and another subspecies, suspected to be ancestral to all other populations, is found in the Fitzroy River system in Queensland. Other common names for golden perch are "goldens", "yellowbelly" and "callop", the last generally used only in South Australia.

## Biology
Under favourable conditions female golden perch are capable of spawning more than once a season . However, without suitable environmental cues they may fail to breed in season, with the eggs being resorbed . Spawning period is from early spring to late autumn . They commonly spawn at night .
Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec. Fecundity ranges from 500,000 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Bony fish, Insects, Lobsters and shrimps & prawns.
It may be targeted by predators such as Maccullochella peelii peelii, Perca fluviatilis and Phalacrocorax sp..
