## Description
The European perch, also known as the common perch, redfin perch, big-scaled redfin, English perch, Euro perch, Eurasian perch, Eurasian river perch, Hatch, poor man's rockfish or in Anglophone parts of Europe, simply the perch, is a predatory freshwater fish native to Europe and North Asia. It is the type species of the genus Perca.

## Biology
During breeding, males arrive at the spawning area ahead of the females. One or two of these males chases a ripe female as soon as it arrives in the area (polyandry) . The queue of males maybe longer composed of about 15 to 25 individuals, but only two prod their snouts against the female's belly . After rounds of curved course through the interlacing branches near the surface , males fertilize the egg ribbon as the female lays them over weeds or other submerged objects . Eggs grouped in long white ribbons (up to 1 m) are found over submerged objects . Eggs hatch in about 8 to 16 days at normal temperatures . Males mature at 2-3 years and females at 4 years. Spawning, in the Northern hemisphere, happens in spring in waters with temperatures between 7-8°C. Eggs are laid in sticky strings becoming fixed to aquatic plants and rocks. Incubation lasts about 1- 8 days at 1- 3°. Egg size 2.0-2.5mm, larval length at hatching 5mm.
Spawning occurs mainly in Mar, Apr, May, Jun. Fecundity ranges from 1,698-47,580 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Bony fish, Cladocerans, Copepods, Crabs, Fish eggs & larvae, Insects, Isopods, Mysids, Non-annelids and shrimps & prawns.
It may be targeted by predators such as Anguilla anguilla, Esox lucius, Lota lota, Lutra lutra, Perca fluviatilis, Salmo trutta trutta, Sander lucioperca and Silurus glanis.
