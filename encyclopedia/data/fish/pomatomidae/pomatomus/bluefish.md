## Description
The bluefish is the only extant species of the family Pomatomidae. It is a marine pelagic fish found around the world in temperate and subtropical waters, except for the northern Pacific Ocean. Bluefish are known as tailor in Australia and New Zealand, elf and shad in South Africa. It is a popular gamefish and food fish.

## Biology
Fecundity varies from 400,000 to 2,000,000 eggs depending on the size of the individual (ranging from 370,000 in a 31 cm fish to 1,240,000 in a 54 cm fish) . Tailor are serial spawners .
Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec. Fecundity ranges from 114,513-1,000,000 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Bony fish, Copepods, Crabs, Gastropods, Insects, Isopods, Polychaetes, Shrimps & prawns, Squids/cuttlefish and terrestrial plants.
It may be targeted by predators such as Alopias vulpinus, Argyrosomus hololepidotus, Atractoscion aequidens, Carcharhinus limbatus, Carcharhinus plumbeus, Carcharodon carchari, Galeocerdo cavier, Isurus oxyrinchus, Isurus paucus, Morone saxatilis, Petromyzon marinus, Pomatomus saltatrix, Prionace glauca, Sarda sarda, Squatina dumeril, Thunnus thynnus, Triakis megalopterus and Xiphias gladius.
