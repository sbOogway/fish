The brown trout is a species of carnivorous ray-finned fish and the most widely distributed species of the salmonid genus Salmo, endemic to most of Europe, West Asia and parts of North Africa, and has been widely introduced globally as a game fish, even becoming one of the world's worst invasive species outside of its native range.

## Habitat
Brown Trout is a freshwater species. It is targeted across much of its range, with fishing conditions varying by season and locality.

## Seasonality
Its most productive fishing months in the Northern Hemisphere are typically Mar, Apr, May, Sep, Oct, Nov, though exact timing depends on local climate and water temperature.
