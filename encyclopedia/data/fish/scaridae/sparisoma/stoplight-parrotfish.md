## Description
The stoplight parrotfish is a species of marine ray-finned fish, a parrotfish from the family Scaridae, inhabiting coral reefs in Florida, Caribbean Sea, Gulf of Mexico, Bermuda and as far south as Brazil. It mainly feeds on algae by scraping and excavating it with its teeth. Like most parrotfish, it is able to change sex.

## Biology
The type of reproductive behavior is related to the color phase of the males involved.
Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Oct, Nov, Dec.
Its diet consists mainly of benthic algae/weeds.
It may be targeted by predators such as Caranx ruber and Lutjanus jocu.
