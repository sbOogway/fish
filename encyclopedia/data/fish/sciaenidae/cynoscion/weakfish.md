## Description
Cynoscion regalis, the weakfish, is a marine ray-finned fish of the family Sciaenidae, the drums and croakers.

## Biology
Spawning occurs mainly in May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep. Fecundity ranges from 44,880-1,725,920 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Bivalves, Bony fish, Copepods, Crabs, Mysids, Polychaetes, Shrimps & prawns and stomatopods.
It may be targeted by predators such as Carcharhinus obscurus, Cynoscion regalis, Morone saxatilis, Paralichthys dentatus, Petromyzon marinus, Pomatomus saltatrix and Raja eglanteria.
