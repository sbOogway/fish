## Description
The wahoo is a scombrid fish found worldwide in tropical and subtropical seas. In Hawaii, the wahoo is known as ono. The species is sometimes called hoo in the United States. It is best known to sports fishermen, as its speed and high quality makes it a prized and valued game fish.

## Biology
Spawning seems to extend over a long period; fish in different maturity stages are frequently caught at the same time.
Spawning occurs mainly in May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct. Fecundity ranges from 6,000,000 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Benthic algae/weeds, Bony fish and squids/cuttlefish.
It may be targeted by predators such as Carcharhinus albimarginatus.
