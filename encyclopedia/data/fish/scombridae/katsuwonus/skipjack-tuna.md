## Description
The skipjack tuna is a perciform fish in the tuna family, Scombridae, and is the only member of the genus Katsuwonus. It is a cosmopolitan pelagic fish found in tropical and warm-temperate waters. Skipjack tuna can grow up to 1.1 m in length, and is a very important species for fisheries.

## Biology
In tropical waters, reproductively active female skipjack tuna spawn almost daily.
Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec. Fecundity ranges from 80,000-2,000,000 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Bony fish, Euphausiids, Fish eggs & larvae, Lobsters, Octopi, Shrimps & prawns and squids/cuttlefish.
It may be targeted by predators such as Acanthocybium solandri, Anous stolidus, Carcharhinus albimarginatus, Coryphaena hippurus, Isurus oxyrinchus, Katsuwonus pelamis, Makaira indica, Makaira mazara, Makaira nigricans, Sterna fuscata, Thunnus albacares, Thunnus obesus, billfishes and whaler sharks.
