## Description
Pagrus major, the red seabream, red pargo, red porgy or silver seabream, is a species of marine ray-finned fish in the family Sparidae, which includes the seabreams and porgies. This species is found in the Western Pacific Ocean. The fish has high culinary and cultural importance in Japan, and is also frequently eaten in Korea and Taiwan.

## Biology
Conflicting descriptions of the reproductive style of this species have been reported, e.g., Ref. 34228 describe this species as being a gonochorist . After clarificatory analyses on the protogynous characteristics of this species, gonochorism is confirmed .
Spawning occurs mainly in Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug. Fecundity ranges from 228,996-4,544,948 eggs.
Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Bony fish, Copepods, Crabs, Fish eggs & larvae, Jellyfish/hydroids, Non-annelids, Ostracods and polychaetes.
