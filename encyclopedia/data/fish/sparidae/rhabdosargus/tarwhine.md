## Description
Rhabdosargus sarba, also known as the goldlined seabream, silver bream, tarwhine, or yellowfin bream, is a species of marine ray-finned fish belonging to the family Sparidae, which includes the seabreams and porgies. This fish has a wide Indo-Pacific distribution.

## Biology
Normally sexes are separate but some individuals are protandrous due to geographical variation in sexual pattern . Gonochorism is confirmed in Australia, and protandry in Asia . Also Ref. 28504.
Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec.
Its diet consists mainly of benthic algae/weeds.
It may be targeted by predators such as Carcharhinus limbatus, Ceryle rudis, Hydroprogne tschegrava and Phalacrocorax carbo.
