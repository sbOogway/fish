# Spinning Rod

A spinning rod is the backbone of the spinning setup. It's designed for use with a spinning reel (mounted below the rod) and features line guides that face downward.

## Key Specs

- **Length**: Determines casting distance and leverage. Longer = more distance, shorter = more precision.
- **Power**: How much force is needed to bend the rod. Match to your target species.
- **Action**: Where the rod bends. Fast action (tip-heavy) gives better sensitivity; moderate action spreads the load for treble-hooked lures.
- **Material**: Carbon fiber offers the best sensitivity-to-weight ratio; fiberglass is more durable; composite blends both.
