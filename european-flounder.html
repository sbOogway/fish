Distribution

Description

The European flounder is a flatfish of European coastal waters from the White Sea in the north to the Mediterranean and the Black Sea in the south. It has been introduced into the United States and Canada accidentally through transport in ballast water. It is caught and used for human consumption.

Biology

Spawns in the water column . Produces 400,000 to 2,000,000 eggs for each female . Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May. Fecundity ranges from 248,948-2,088,414 eggs. Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Bivalves, Bony fish, Gastropods, Isopods, Ostracods, Polychaetes and shrimps & prawns. It may be targeted by predators such as Ardea cinerea, Esox lucius, Halichoerus grypus, Mergus merganser, Perca fluviatilis, Phalacrocorax carbo, Phoca vitulina, Phocoena phocoena, Pleurobrachia pileus and Sander lucioperca.

Behavior

Adults occur on mud and sand bottom in shallow water, at sea and brackish; often entering freshwaters . During winter, adults retreat to deeper, warmer waters, where they spawn in spring . The growing larvae are moving to the coast . Larvae and early juveniles use selective tidal transport to migrate upstream rivers using a range of triggers such as salinity, prey density and water temperature . Nocturnal and burrowing .
