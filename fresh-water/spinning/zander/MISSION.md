# Mission: Zander Spinning (Fresh Water)

## Why
I want to learn how to catch zander using spinning tackle. I am a complete beginner — I have access to both rivers and lakes, and I want to build the skills to confidently target zander in either environment.

## Success looks like
- I can set up a spinning rod and reel correctly for zander fishing
- I can choose the right lure and retrieve technique for the conditions
- I can read the water to identify likely zander spots
- I can catch a zander on my own

## Constraints
- Complete beginner — start from fundamentals
- Access to both rivers and lakes
- Fresh water only for this track

## Out of scope
- Salt water fishing (covered in `salt-water/`)
- Other fresh water species (add new tracks as needed)
- Advanced tournament techniques
