Distribution

Gray Snapper inhabits Freshwater, Brackish, Saltwater waters. Map shows fishbase occurrence records.

Description

The mangrove snapper or gray snapper is a species of snapper native to the western Atlantic Ocean from Massachusetts to Brazil, the Gulf of Mexico, Bermuda, and the Caribbean Sea. The species can be found in a wide variety of habitats, including brackish and fresh waters. It is commercially important and is sought as a game fish. It can also be found in the aquarium trade.

Size

Typical length is 40 cm, with a maximum recorded length of 89 cm.

Weight

Maximum recorded weight is 20.0 kg.

Age

Lifespan is around 21 years.

Sexual maturity is reached at about 3 years of age.

Biology

Spawning occurs in summer near the time of the full moon. Spawning occurs mainly in Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct. Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Bivalves, Bony fish, Crabs, Gastropods, Isopods, Lobsters, Mysids, Octopi, Polychaetes, Shrimps & prawns and stomatopods. It may be targeted by predators such as Gymnothorax funebris, Gymnothorax moringa and Sphyraena barracuda.

Seasonality

Its most productive fishing months in the Northern Hemisphere are typically Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec, though exact timing depends on local climate and water temperature.

Conservation

IUCN conservation status: LC.
