Description

The haddock is a saltwater ray-finned fish from the family Gadidae, the true cods. It is the only species in the monotypic genus Melanogrammus. It is found in the North Atlantic Ocean and associated seas, where it is an important species for fisheries, especially in northern Europe, where it is marketed fresh, frozen and smoked; smoked varieties include the Finnan haddie and the Arbroath smokie. Other smoked versions include long boneless, the filleted side of larger haddock smoked in oak chips with the skin left on the fillet.

Biology

Oviparous, sexes are separate . Spawning occurs in typically marine waters between 50 and 150 m depth . Spawning occurs mainly in Mar, Apr, May, Jun. Fecundity ranges from 59,900-1,841,000 eggs. Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Ascidians, Benth. copepods, Benthic algae/weeds, Bivalves, Bony fish, Chitons, Cladocerans, Copepods, Crabs, Euphausiids, Fish eggs & larvae, Gastropods, Insects, Isopods, Jellyfish/hydroids, Lobsters, Mysids, Non-annelids, Octopi, Ostracods, Plank. copepods, Polychaetes, Sea cucumbers, Sea stars/brittle stars, Sea urchins, Shrimps & prawns, Sponges and squids/cuttlefish. It may be targeted by predators such as Amblyraja radiata, Balaenoptera acutorostrata, Chelidonichthys gurnardus, Conger conger, Cystophora cristata, Fulmarus glacialis, Gadus morhua, Halichoerus grypus, Hippoglossoides platessoides, Lophius americanus, Lophius piscatorius, Melanogrammus aeglefinus, Mergus merganser, Merlangius merlangus, Micromesistius poutassou, Myxine glutinosa, Petromyzon marinus, Phoca groenlandica, Phoca vitulina, Phocoena phocoena, Pollachius virens, Pomatomus saltatrix, Somniosus microcephalus, Squalus acanthias, Thunnus albacares, Tursiops truncatus, Urophycis chuss and Xiphias gladius.

Behavior

Adults are found more commonly from 80 to 200 m, over rock, sand, gravel or shells, usually at temperatures between 4° and 10°C.
