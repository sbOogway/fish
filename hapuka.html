Description

The hāpuku, hapuka or whapuku, also known as groper, is a wreckfish of the family Polyprionidae, found around southern Australia, southern South America, South Africa, Tristan da Cunha and New Zealand at depths between 30 and 800 m. Its length is between 60 and 180 cm, and it can weigh up to 100 kg. It is sometimes described locally as cod, although that properly refers to other fish.

Biology

Are primary gonochorists . Spawning occurs mainly in Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep. Fecundity ranges from 1,500,000-5,800,000 eggs. Its diet consists mainly of Bony fish, Lobsters, Squids/cuttlefish and stomatopods. It may be targeted by predators such as Sperm whale.

Behavior

Adults occur generally over rough ground from the central shelf (about 100 m) to the shelf edge and down to the upper slope.
