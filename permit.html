Distribution

Description

The permit is a game fish of the western Atlantic Ocean belonging to the family Trachinotidae. Adults feed on crabs, shrimp, and smaller fish.

Biology

Spawning occurs mainly in May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Dec. Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Crabs, Gastropods, Sea urchins and shrimps & prawns.

Behavior

Adults frequently in channels or holes, over sandy flats, around reefs, and at times over mud bottoms; usually solitary or in small schools; smaller fish tolerate brackish water. Spawn offshore .
