Distribution

Description

Pollachius pollachius is a species of marine fish in the family Gadidae. FAO uses the English name pollack for this species, whereas in American English it is known as European pollock. Other vernacular names include lythe, and in the Isle of Man, callig.

Size

Typical length is 75 cm, with a maximum recorded length of 130 cm.

Weight

Maximum recorded weight is 18.1 kg.

Age

Lifespan is around 8 years.

Sexual maturity is reached at about 3 years of age.

Biology

Oviparous and gonochorous . Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May. Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Benth. copepods, Bony fish, Cladocerans, Copepods, Crabs, Euphausiids, Fish eggs & larvae, Jellyfish/hydroids, Polychaetes, Shrimps & prawns and squids/cuttlefish. It may be targeted by predators such as Myxine glutinosa.

Seasonality

Its most productive fishing months in the Northern Hemisphere are typically Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, Oct, Nov, Dec, though exact timing depends on local climate and water temperature.

Conservation

IUCN conservation status: LC.
