Distribution

Red Drum inhabits Brackish, Saltwater waters. Map shows fishbase occurrence records.

Description

The red drum, also known as redfish, channel bass, puppy drum, spottail bass, or simply red, is a game fish found in the Atlantic Ocean from Massachusetts to Florida and in the Gulf of Mexico from Florida to northern Mexico. It is the only species in the genus Sciaenops.

Size

Typical length is 100 cm, with a maximum recorded length of 155 cm.

Weight

Maximum recorded weight is 45.0 kg.

Age

Lifespan is around 50 years.

Sexual maturity is reached at about 4.5 years of age.

Biology

Spawning occurs mainly in Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov. Fecundity ranges from 3,500,000 eggs. Its diet consists mainly of bony fish.

Seasonality

Its most productive fishing months in the Northern Hemisphere are typically Mar, Apr, May, Sep, Oct, Nov, though exact timing depends on local climate and water temperature.

Conservation

IUCN conservation status: LC.
