Distribution

Sergeant Major inhabits Saltwater waters. Records span a broad geographic range. Map shows fishbase occurrence records.

Description

The sergeant major or píntano is a species of damselfish. It grows to a maximum length of about 22.9 cm (9.0 in).

Size

Typical length is 15 cm, with a maximum recorded length of 23 cm.

Weight

Maximum recorded weight is 0.2 kg.

Biology

Oviparous, distinct pairing during breeding . Eggs are demersal and adhere to the substrate . Males guard and aerate the eggs . Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec. Its diet consists mainly of benthic algae/weeds. It may be targeted by predators such as Cephalopholis cruentata, Epinephelus striatus, Mycteroperca venenosa, Plectropomus leopardus, Rachycentron canadum and Thalassoma bifasciatum.

Seasonality

Its most productive fishing months in the Northern Hemisphere are typically Jan, Feb, Mar, Apr, May, Jun, Jul, Aug, Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec, though exact timing depends on local climate and water temperature.

Conservation
