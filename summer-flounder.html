Distribution

Description

The summer flounder or fluke is a marine flatfish that is found in the Atlantic Ocean off the East Coast of the United States and Canada. It is especially abundant in waters from North Carolina to Massachusetts.

Biology

Distinct pairing . Spawning occurs mainly in Jan, Feb, Dec. Its diet consists mainly of Amphipods, Benth. copepods, Benthic algae/weeds, Bivalves, Bony fish, Copepods, Crabs, Mysids, Polychaetes and shrimps & prawns. It may be targeted by predators such as Cynoscion regalis, Morone saxatilis, Pomatomus saltator, Prionotus carolinus and Squalus acanthias.

Behavior

Adults usually prefer hard sandy substrate where they can burrow; can exploit a broad range of lower and mid-estuary habitats including salt marsh creeks and seagrass beds, which usually have muddy or silty substrates , as well as sand flats .
